# Finished book files for My First Book of Healthy Living

The title folder now contains the completed illustrated book and its standard print, screen-reading and editable editions.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [A5 print interior](My_First_Book_of_Healthy_Living_Print_Interior_A5.pdf) | 61-page A5 book interior, separate front and back matter, chapter-opening page numbers in Contents and ten chapter illustrations. Body text is 14 pt. |
| [A5 two-up printer file](My_First_Book_of_Healthy_Living_Hardcopy_A5_2up.pdf) | 31 landscape sheets, two consecutive A5 pages per sheet, prepared as a print-only file. |

Copyright wording and publisher imprint match Serenity and Tranquillity. The supplied wrap ISBN failed its checksum, and no replacement was supplied. No ISBN placeholder or false barcode appears in the book. A publisher-assigned ISBN and printer-specific full wrap will be needed for commercial printing; the spine width depends on the printer template.

The “Find a Topic” guide is at the end of the book after the adult references and author biography. In the digital editions its entries jump to chapters, and chapter vocabulary links jump to full glossary definitions. This short children's guide uses chapter destinations instead of a scholarly page-number index.
