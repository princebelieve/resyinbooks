# Source-to-working-manuscript map

Professor's original file remains unchanged in `../../Professor_Supplied/Pasted text.txt`. This map records how its ideas were used in the completed child-facing book.

| Supplied idea/material | Working manuscript treatment |
|---|---|
| Dedication, preface, parent/teacher note | Preserved in meaning; rewritten for a child-centred, supportive tone. Added a clear age-use note and a disclaimer that fictional situations are teaching examples. |
| Ch. 1: four dimensions of health, why health matters, Ada story, habits, questions/activity/words/promise | Preserved and expanded as the opening framework; adapted Ada's story around a realistic morning routine. |
| Ch. 2: food/nutrition, carbohydrates, proteins, fruits/vegetables, fats, water, Nigerian meal examples, breakfast, food safety, moderation | Nutrient functions and familiar meal examples retained and explained. Added breakfast examples and food-access sensitivity; avoid rigid plate rules and food shame. |
| Ch. 3: activity types/benefits, team play, screen breaks, safety, 60-minute guidance | Preserved and expanded; added inclusive adaptations and clear stop/seek-adult safety guidance. WHO range is limited to ages 5–17. |
| Ch. 4: handwashing, bathing, teeth, hair/nails, personal items, environment | All topics retained; child-facing hygiene instruction expanded. WHO hand-hygiene and oral-health references added. |
| Ch. 5: check-ups, immunisation, water/food/environment safety, medicines, traditional preparations, malaria | Core material retained; strengthened adult/health-professional boundaries, unknown-substance response and Nigeria-specific referral to NPHCDA. |
| Ch. 6: feelings, stress causes, coping, friendship, help-seeking, self-control | Retained and developed with a bullying/help-seeking scenario, support map and guidance to keep asking adults if the first does not help. |
| Ch. 7: sleep benefits, age bands, routines, rest, effects of too little sleep | Retained; AASM ranges cited; added child-safe language and help-seeking rather than blame. |
| Ch. 8: school, work, play, family, time use, chores, play benefits, overloaded schedules, digital balance | Preserved as a balance chapter; title clarified to “chores”; explicitly states chores must be safe and age-appropriate, never adult work or a replacement for school. |
| Ch. 9: daily/morning routines, water, healthy activities, harmful habits, responsible choices, consistency | Retained and refocused as a practical habit-building chapter with a kind, non-punitive tracker; substance-safety guidance retained. |
| Ch. 10: personal health, relationships, safety, environment, health ambassador, healthy future | Retained; reframed around one supervised community project and the boundary that adults remain responsible for safety and larger problems. |
| Conclusion, pledge, checklist, glossary, revision questions, references, author bio | Kept; checklist changed to a reflective habit tracker to avoid scoring or shaming children. Glossary and revision questions were expanded to match the revised text. Author bio wording is retained verbatim and remains subject to author confirmation. |
| Cover promise of lessons, questions, activities, healthy promises, practical tips | Built into chapter pattern; chapter situations and activities vary. The ten full-size user-supplied chapter illustrations were selected and integrated; see `Illustration_Selection_Record.md`. |

The final child-facing manuscript is `My_First_Book_of_Healthy_Living_Final_Manuscript.md`; delivered editions are listed in `../For_Professor/README.md`. A qualified Nigerian child-health review and publisher ISBN/printer-template details remain production gates for commercial release.
