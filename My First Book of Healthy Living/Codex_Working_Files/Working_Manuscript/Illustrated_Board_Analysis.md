## Image-quality/use decision

The native card crops are useful as chapter-specific visual references and proofing aids. Each panel is about 255 × 415 pixels and contains small embedded text. Keep the crops in working files; do not enlarge them into print-page art or reuse their embedded text as the book's readable body copy. The final illustrated interior should use original full-resolution chapter illustrations based on these exact scenes and messages, with page text typeset separately.
