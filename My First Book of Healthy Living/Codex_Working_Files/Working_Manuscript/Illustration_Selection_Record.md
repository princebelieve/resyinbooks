# Illustration Selection Record

## Decision

Use the ten full-size WebP chapter illustrations supplied by the user in `Illustration_References/mine/`, in numerical order, for the illustrated review copy. They align with the supplied ten-chapter storyboard and provide clearer, more complete child-centered scenes than the small storyboard crops. Selected copies are in `Selected_Illustrations/`; source WebP files remain preserved in `Illustration_References/mine/`.

## Per-chapter match

1. Understanding healthy living — four connected parts of well-being and a habit activity.
2. Eating well — food groups, balanced meal examples, hydration and an activity.
3. Exercise, play and physical fitness — movement, benefits, reflection and a weekly plan.
4. Personal hygiene and cleanliness — handwashing, teeth, clean clothing and surroundings.
5. Preventive healthcare — vaccination, safe water/food, mosquito-net use, hazards and getting adult/clinical help.
6. Managing stress, feelings and emotions — naming feelings, calming choices and help-seeking.
7. Adequate sleep and rest — a bedtime setting, sleep habits and routine activity.
8. Balancing school, work, play and family life — study, chores, active play, family time and balance.
9. Healthy daily habits and responsible choices — food, water, exercise, hygiene, safe choices and a habit chart.
10. Personal well-being and a healthy future — caring for self, others and community.
