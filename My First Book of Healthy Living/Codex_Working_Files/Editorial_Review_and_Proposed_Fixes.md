# My First Book of Healthy Living - Completed Book

## Editorial status

The child-facing manuscript has been developed from Professor's supplied material into a complete ten-chapter book for readers approximately 7-12. The original source remains unchanged in Professor_Supplied. The completed master is in Codex_Working_Files/Working_Manuscript/My_First_Book_of_Healthy_Living_Final_Manuscript.md.

The work preserves the ten subject areas and the author's intended message while correcting the child-facing structure and health guidance. It includes Nigerian food examples, short child-centred situations, practical activities, reflection questions, a pledge, glossary, revision questions, adult references and the preserved author biography.

## Comparison with Nigerian children's books

This is a topic-led health guide, not a fictional chapter-book series. It uses relevant Nigerian children's publishing patterns without borrowing another book's text or characters:

- Yinka Washes His Hands by Fatima Akilu (Cassava Republic, 2009) teaches hygiene through a concrete child-centred story and illustration. This book uses short situations inside each lesson to ground the health guidance in children's lives. Bibliographic record: https://books.google.com/books?id=AzYlMwEACAAJ
- Too Small Tola by Atinuke, illustrated by Onyinye Iwu, is presented for ages 7-9 and uses manageable stories, humour, Nigerian settings and realistic illustrations. This is a useful local-reader benchmark for clear language and recognisable children; this guide keeps a wider 7-12 age band because it includes ten health topics and activities. Publisher information: https://www.penguinrandomhouseretail.com/book/?isbn=9781536230376
- Why Do You Wash Your Hands? is a Nigerian illustrated hygiene book with English, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa editions. It shows why clear visual lessons and family reading matter for health education. IweMi page: https://iwemi.org/books/why-do-you-wash-your-hands
- Wellness Champions is a recent Nigerian child-health/nutrition title with activities and a school-facing programme. This manuscript adds child activities, self-reflection and Nigerian food examples while covering ten areas beyond nutrition. BellaNaija review: https://www.bellanaija.com/2024/07/bn-book-review-wellness-champion-by-eziaha-bolaji-olojo-and-sons-review-by-tosin-sanni/

These are design benchmarks, not rankings or claims of equivalent production polish. The comparisons checked age fit, child-centred examples, local context and illustration-led learning.

## Health-source checks

The book's adult reference section cites WHO physical-activity guidance for ages 5-17; WHO/UNICEF community hand-hygiene guidelines (2025); WHO oral-health, healthy-diet and malaria guidance; AASM pediatric sleep recommendations; WHO mhGAP as background for adult support boundaries; and Nigeria's NPHCDA immunisation information.

The source record and limitations are in Working_Manuscript/Health_Source_Notes.md. Before commercial publication, a qualified Nigerian child-health reviewer should check the health content and the publisher should confirm current local immunisation and malaria guidance.

## Comparison with Serenity and Tranquillity

The handoff mirrors that completed title's usable editions: A5 print interior, A5 two-up printer copy, A4 screen-readable PDF, reflowable EPUB, editable Word master and standalone front-cover JPEG. After increasing the child-facing type and adding end matter, the A5 interior has 61 pages and 31 landscape two-up sheets; the A4 screen edition has 49 pages. Digital contents, vocabulary, chapter-end controls and topic-finder entries have internal links.

The book uses a linked topic finder rather than a subject index with page locators, which suits this short children's guide. The supplied cover's printed ISBN failed its ISBN-13 check. A publisher-assigned print/digital ISBN and printer-specific full wrap are needed before commercial print packaging; these are not prose placeholders in the delivered book.
