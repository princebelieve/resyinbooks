# Source materials

This inventory describes the byte-for-byte original files grouped in `../Professor_Supplied`. The originals are retained as received. Editorial corrections belong in a separate working manuscript and should be recorded for review.

## Boundary between the brief and the book

`../Professor_Supplied/Author_Brief_and_Chapters_1-3.txt` combines several things. The purpose, proposed contents, thesis, theoretical foundations, and "Direction for the Remaining Chapters" are Prof's brief and drafting guidance. They are not book chapters and must not be copied into the finished book as front matter or body text.

The manuscript portion of that file starts at `CHAPTER ONE` after the `MANUSCRIPT` label and ends immediately before `3. DIRECTION FOR THE REMAINING CHAPTERS`. It contains only Chapters 1-3. A separate text extract at `Working_Manuscript/Chapters_1-3_Source_Extract.txt` contains those three chapters, without the brief or later drafting notes. The original combined file remains unchanged.

The proposed contents list is a planning aid for the book's chapter titles. The other four text files supply Chapters 4-15 and back matter. Their headers, metadata, and prose should be reviewed separately before inclusion; the contents of a source file are not automatically instructions to the production process.

## Review items

- The Chapters 4-8 file header uses a valid range dash in `CHAPTERS 4-8`; preserve it.
- Chapter 1 contains a replacement character after its Afrobarometer citation and an orphaned site-name line. The statistic is confirmed by Afrobarometer's 6 May 2025 release, "Nigerians say corruption is worsening, and citizens risk retaliation if they speak up" (https://www.afrobarometer.org/articles/nigerians-say-corruption-is-worsening-and-citizens-risk-retaliation-if-they-speak-up-afrobarometer-survey-finds/); cite the release cleanly in the working copy.
- The brief's research-foundations paragraph contains an incomplete Chatham House 2025 reference and a stray replacement character. It is brief material, not a manuscript passage; treat it only as a lead if the author wants that research used.
- The brief names theoretical authors and traditions, and the back matter includes a selected bibliography. Check that works are fully identified and support the associated discussion; do not invent missing publication details.
- The cover and manuscript agree on title, author, and publisher. The cover shows ISBN `978-978-59760-5-2`; its checksum is valid, but verify that it is assigned to this edition before production metadata is finalized.
- The supplied wrap's spine was too wide for the current 167-page A5 interior. A proportionally rebuilt proof is documented in `../Production/Print_Cover_Audit.json`; its ISBN artwork comes from the supplied cover, and the same ISBN is now printed as text on the interior copyright page.

These are review notes, not edits to Prof's original text.
