# Files for Prof

This folder contains one copy of each current book deliverable. The 167-page A5 full-wrap proof is included for review only; its spine uses a cream-paper assumption and needs the selected printer's template and stock specifications before printing.

- The A5 single-page interior is the 167-page print edition.
- Its two-page Contents lists all 15 full chapter titles and their opening page numbers.
- The A5 two-up PDF is the same interior imposed as 84 landscape spreads, with a blank final page; print files contain no interactive annotations.
- The digital PDF and EPUB retain navigable full chapter titles; the EPUB is a single-column reflowable list for phones.
- The editable Word file and standalone front-cover JPEG are separate formats for editing or distribution.
- `Cover_Spread_A5_167p_Cream_Assumption.pdf` and `.jpg` are review proofs, not printer-approved covers.
