# Content preservation audit

## Editorial approach

Prof's supplied manuscript is the book text. It was assembled in source order; it was not rewritten from the author brief or from *Serenity and Tranquillity*. The earlier book was used as a production-workflow reference only. Chapter organization, section numbering and arguments were not consolidated, recast or renumbered.

## Comparison results

- All 1,892 chapter source lines emitted by the source-boundary parser matched the editable Word manuscript line for line after the documented damaged-citation repair.
- All 158 numbered section headings are retained in order.
- All 375 back-matter lines/cells matched the defined assembly output after the documented bibliography cleanup, added citation and removal of the embedded editorial instruction.
- The fifteen policy recommendations were changed from source lines to a Word table without changing their text or order.

## Working-copy edits

1. The Chapter One Afrobarometer line ended in a replacement character and a detached `afrobarometer.org` string. That damaged tail was replaced by `(Afrobarometer, 6 May 2025)`, and the full release reference was added to the bibliography.
2. One repeated Sen (1999) entry and one repeated North (1990) entry were each listed once in the editable manuscript. Both original repeated entries remain in the raw source file.
3. An editorial sentence in the supplied bibliography asking that references be checked was omitted from book text and recorded in the editorial log.
4. The proposal, author brief, proposed contents/theory notes, “Direction for the Remaining Chapters,” installment instructions and post-manuscript production notes were treated as guidance or file notes, not chapters.

The source materials remain unchanged. Formatting adds a title page, refreshable contents, indexes, links and page layout; it does not paraphrase Prof's prose. See [Editorial Change Log](Editorial_Change_Log.md) and [Bibliography Audit](Bibliography_Audit.md) for outstanding review queries.
