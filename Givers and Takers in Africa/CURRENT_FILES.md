## Which cover is which?

- **Supplied full-wrap reference (back + spine + front):** [Givers and Takers Book Cover.jpeg](Professor_Supplied/Givers%20and%20Takers%20Book%20Cover.jpeg). This is Prof's source artwork.
- **Generated 167-page A5 wrap proof (back + spine + front):** [PDF](For_Professor/Cover_Spread_A5_167p_Cream_Assumption.pdf) and [JPEG](For_Professor/Cover_Spread_A5_167p_Cream_Assumption.jpg). Its spine assumes cream paper; it is a working proof for review, not printer-approved. Confirm the printer's paper, binding, trim and template before use.
- **Standalone digital front cover:** [Givers_and_Takers_in_Africa_Cover_Front.jpg](For_Professor/Givers_and_Takers_in_Africa_Cover_Front.jpg), in the professor delivery folder. It is front-only, not a full wrap.
