# Final files for *Givers and Takers in Africa*

Only the current usable editions are kept in this folder. The full production history and future-book checklist are in [Book_Production_Workflow.md](../Book_Production_Workflow.md). Deleted source and working files remain recoverable from Git history.

The subject index is at the end of each edition, after all supplied back matter. The print ISBN also appears as a barcode on the supplied back-cover art. No final full-wrap print cover is included: obtain the printer's paper, binding, trim and cover template before producing one.
