# Official examination source notes

Checked on 14 November 2026 for intake planning:

- WAEC's [English Language e-learning page](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/English/Englmain.html) says its resources provide standards and examiner performance commentary. The page presently exposes selected examination resources through 2023, so it is useful for studying past examiner feedback but does not establish the format of a future sitting.
- WAEC Nigeria's [entry regulations](https://waecnigeria.org/node/102) describe its 2026–2027 core-subject arrangements. This is not an English Language paper specification.
- NECO's [official site](https://neco.gov.ng/) publishes series-specific guidelines, notices and timetables. Use the notice and timetable for the reader's own examination series rather than assuming WAEC and NECO share the same timing or paper codes.

No current official syllabus document establishing every paper's structure was located during intake. Keep exact durations, marks, paper numbers, item counts and current syllabus claims out of the book until checked against primary official materials for the relevant series. Past questions and examiner reports should be clearly dated and attributed. Questions described as original practice must not be relabeled as actual past questions.
