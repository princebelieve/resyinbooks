# Recovered outline supplied for the handbook

**Provisional title:** WAEC/NECO English Language Revision Handbook  
**Author:** Prof. Johnson Agharese Egonmwan

This is the 15-chapter outline supplied in the request. It is preserved as a planning brief, not treated as source manuscript prose. The supplied cover advertises 16 chapters and a separate Chapter Sixteen draft was also received, so the final structure needs to reconcile the discrepancy.

1. **Introduction to English Language** — Importance of English; structure of WAEC/NECO English examination; study and examination techniques.
2. **Grammar (Parts of Speech)** — Nouns; pronouns; verbs; adjectives; adverbs; prepositions; conjunctions; interjections; determiners.
3. **Tenses and Sentence Structure** — The twelve English tenses; active and passive voice; direct and indirect speech; types of sentences; common errors.
4. **Concord (Subject–Verb Agreement)** — Rules of concord; frequent WAEC/NECO questions; practice exercises.
5. **Lexis and Structure** — Synonyms; antonyms; homonyms; homophones; idiomatic expressions; phrasal verbs; practice questions.
6. **Comprehension** — Reading techniques; answering comprehension questions; vocabulary in context; sample passages with solutions.
7. **Summary Writing** — Principles of summary; identifying main ideas; avoiding common mistakes; model answers.
8. **Essay Writing** — Narrative; descriptive; expository; argumentative; article; speech; letter writing.
9. **Letter Writing** — Formal; informal; semi-formal; business letters; sample WAEC/NECO questions.
10. **Oral English** — Vowels; consonants; diphthongs; stress patterns; intonation; minimal pairs; practice tests.
11. **Figures of Speech and Literary Devices** — Simile; metaphor; personification; hyperbole; irony; euphemism; oxymoron; alliteration; assonance; practice questions.
12. **Registers and Idiomatic Expressions** — Legal; medical; banking; sports; ICT; education registers; common idioms.
13. **Vocabulary Development** — Word formation; prefixes; suffixes; root words; confusing words; one-word substitutes.
14. **Examination Practice** — WAEC past questions (2015–2025); NECO past questions (2015–2025); detailed explanations; marking guide.
15. **Mock Examination and Success Guide** — Two complete mock examinations; answers and explanations; common examination mistakes; final revision checklist; tips for scoring A1.
