14.1 Introduction

The English Language examination requires adequate preparation, constant practice and effective time management. This chapter provides model questions similar in style and standard to those used in WAEC and NECO.

Note: These are original practice questions designed for revision and are not reproduced past examination papers.

Section A: Lexis and Structure

Choose the option that best completes each sentence.

1. Neither the principal nor the teachers ________ present at the meeting.

A. was

B. were

C. is

D. has

Answer: B

Explanation: The verb agrees with the subject nearest to it ("teachers"), which is plural.

Section E: Comprehension

Passage

Amina was always punctual and hardworking. She believed that success depended on discipline and perseverance. Instead of wasting her evenings, she devoted her time to reading and completing her assignments. Her determination eventually earned her a national scholarship.

Questions

1. Why was Amina successful?

Answer: Because she was disciplined, hardworking and persevering.

2. What reward did she receive?

Answer: She received a national scholarship.

3. What does the word perseverance mean in the passage?

Answer: Determination despite difficulties.

Section F: Summary Writing

Passage

Successful farmers prepare their land early, use improved seeds, apply fertilisers appropriately and protect their crops from pests. They also keep proper farm records to improve productivity.

Questions

1. In one sentence, state how successful farmers improve productivity.

Suggested Answer

Successful farmers improve productivity by preparing their land early, using improved seeds, applying fertilisers properly, protecting crops from pests and keeping accurate farm records.

Model Essay Outline

Topic

The Importance of Integrity Among Nigerian Youths

Introduction

- Meaning of integrity.
- Importance of honesty in society.

Body

- Builds trust.
- Promotes good leadership.
- Reduces corruption.
- Encourages responsible citizenship.
- Improves national image.

Conclusion

Integrity is a priceless virtue that every young Nigerian should cultivate for personal success and national development.

Common Examination Mistakes

Students often:

- Misread questions.
- Ignore instructions.
- Write illegibly.
- Mismanage time.
- Use poor grammar.
- Fail to revise their work.
- Leave questions unanswered.

---

Final Revision Tips

- Revise every topic systematically.
- Practise English every day.
- Read widely to improve vocabulary.
- Solve many original practice questions.
- Develop confidence through regular revision.
- Sleep well before the examination.
- Arrive early at the examination venue.
- Read instructions carefully before answering.

Chapter Summary

Success in WAEC and NECO English Language depends on thorough preparation, consistent practice and effective examination techniques. Students who master grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, summary writing, essay writing, letter writing and Oral English will be well prepared to achieve excellent grades.

---

Comprehensive Revision Exercise

Answer the following under examination conditions.

Objective Questions

1. Choose the correct option to complete each sentence.
2. Choose words nearest and opposite in meaning.
3. Identify the correct idiomatic expressions.
4. Answer Oral English questions.

Theory Questions

1. Write a formal letter on a given topic.
2. Write a 450-word essay on one of the prescribed topics.
3. Answer one comprehension passage.
4. Answer one summary passage.

Time Allowed: 2 hours 30 minutes.

Instruction: Attempt all objective questions and all compulsory theory questions.

WAEC/NECO Success Checklist

Before the examination, ensure that you can:

- ✓ Apply the rules of grammar correctly.
- ✓ Use appropriate vocabulary and idiomatic expressions.
- ✓ Answer comprehension questions accurately.
- ✓ Write concise summaries in your own words.
- ✓ Produce well-organised essays and letters.
- ✓ Recognise English speech sounds and stress patterns.
- ✓ Manage your examination time effectively.
- ✓ Proofread your answers before submission.

Consistent study, regular practice and confidence are the keys to outstanding performance in WAEC and NECO English Language examinations.
