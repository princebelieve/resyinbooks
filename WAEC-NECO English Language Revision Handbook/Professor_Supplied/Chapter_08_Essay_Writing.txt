Chapter Eight

Essay Writing

Learning Objectives

At the end of this chapter, students should be able to:

- Explain the meaning and importance of essay writing.
- Identify the different types of essays tested in WAEC and NECO.
- Organise ideas into clear paragraphs.
- Write effective introductions, body paragraphs and conclusions.
- Apply the principles of good English in essay writing.
- Produce well-developed essays capable of earning high marks.

---

8.1 Meaning of Essay Writing

An essay is a piece of continuous writing in which a writer presents ideas, facts, opinions or experiences on a given topic in a logical and organised manner.

Essay writing enables candidates to demonstrate their command of grammar, vocabulary, organisation of ideas and creativity.

---

8.2 Importance of Essay Writing

Essay writing helps students to:

- Develop effective communication skills.
- Express ideas clearly and logically.
- Improve grammar and vocabulary.
- Develop critical and creative thinking.
- Prepare for school and public examinations.
- Enhance confidence in written communication.

---

8.3 Features of a Good Essay

A good essay should have:

- A suitable title.
- A clear introduction.
- Well-organised body paragraphs.
- Logical presentation of ideas.
- Correct grammar and punctuation.
- Appropriate vocabulary.
- Smooth transitions between paragraphs.
- A strong conclusion.

---

8.4 Stages in Essay Writing

Stage One: Understand the Topic

Read the question carefully and identify exactly what is required.

Stage Two: Brainstorm

Write down relevant ideas before you begin.

Stage Three: Prepare an Outline

Arrange your ideas in logical order.

Stage Four: Write the First Draft

Develop your introduction, body and conclusion.

Stage Five: Revise

Correct grammatical errors, spelling mistakes and punctuation before submitting your work.

---

8.5 Types of Essays

(A) Narrative Essay

A narrative essay tells a story or recounts an event.

Examples:

- An unforgettable day in my life.
- How I spent my last holiday.
- A journey I will never forget.

Guidelines

- Present events in chronological order.
- Use mainly the past tense.
- Include characters, setting and sequence of events.
- End with a meaningful conclusion.

---

(B) Descriptive Essay

A descriptive essay gives a vivid picture of a person, place, object or event.

Examples:

- My School.
- A Market Scene.
- My Favourite Teacher.

Guidelines

- Appeal to the reader's senses.
- Use descriptive adjectives.
- Present details logically.

---

(C) Expository Essay

An expository essay explains or informs.

Examples:

- The Importance of Education.
- Causes of Youth Unemployment.
- Advantages of Modern Technology.

Guidelines

- Explain clearly.
- Support ideas with relevant facts.
- Avoid unnecessary personal opinions.

---

(D) Argumentative Essay

An argumentative essay presents arguments for and against an issue before reaching a logical conclusion.

Examples:

- Should School Uniforms Be Compulsory?
- Social Media Does More Harm Than Good.
- Democracy Is Better Than Military Rule.

Guidelines

- Present both sides fairly.
- Support arguments with evidence and examples.
- End with a convincing conclusion.

---

(E) Article Writing

An article is written for publication in a newspaper, magazine or school magazine.

Features include:

- Attractive title.
- Name of writer where required.
- Clear introduction.
- Informative body.
- Logical conclusion.

---

(F) Speech Writing

A speech is prepared to be delivered before an audience.

Features include:

- Appropriate salutation.
- Introduction of the topic.
- Clear body.
- Conclusion with appreciation.

Example opening:

The Chairman, distinguished guests, teachers and fellow students, good morning.

---

8.6 Paragraph Development

Each paragraph should contain:

- A topic sentence.
- Supporting ideas.
- Relevant examples where necessary.
- A concluding sentence.

One paragraph should normally contain one major idea.

---

8.7 Common Errors in Essay Writing

Avoid:

- Poor handwriting.
- Grammatical errors.
- Wrong spellings.
- Slang and informal expressions.
- Repetition of ideas.
- Poor paragraphing.
- Writing off the topic.
- Failure to revise your work.

---

8.8 Examination Tips

- Choose the essay topic you understand best.
- Spend a few minutes preparing an outline.
- Allocate your time wisely.
- Write legibly.
- Use correct grammar and punctuation.
- Support your ideas with relevant examples.
- Leave a few minutes to revise your work.

---

8.9 Sample Essay Outline

Topic:

The Importance of Education

Introduction

- Meaning of education.
- Importance to individuals and society.

Body

- Reduces poverty.
- Creates employment opportunities.
- Promotes national development.
- Encourages responsible citizenship.
- Improves scientific and technological advancement.

Conclusion

Education is a powerful tool for personal growth and national development. Every individual should therefore value and pursue quality education.

---

Chapter Summary

Essay writing is a vital component of the WAEC and NECO English Language examinations. Success depends on understanding the topic, organising ideas logically, writing clearly and revising carefully before submission.

WAEC/NECO Revision Questions

Objective Questions

1. A narrative essay primarily:
   a. explains
   b. argues
   c. tells a story
   d. advertises

2. Which essay type presents both sides of an issue?
   a. Narrative
   b. Descriptive
   c. Argumentative
   d. Informal

3. A good essay should begin with:
   a. the conclusion
   b. the body
   c. a clear introduction
   d. an apology

4. Which of the following should be avoided in essay writing?
   a. Logical organisation
   b. Correct grammar
   c. Poor paragraphing
   d. Revision

5. Which stage comes before writing the first draft?
   a. Revision
   b. Brainstorming and outlining
   c. Submission
   d. Proofreading only

---

Practical Exercise

Write an essay of about 450 words on one of the following topics:

1. The Importance of Discipline in Schools.
2. An Experience I Will Never Forget.
3. Social Media: A Blessing or a Curse?
4. The Role of Agriculture in Nigeria's Economic Development.
5. My Greatest Ambition in Life.

---

WAEC/NECO Examination Tips

- Read all the essay questions before making your choice.
- Stick to the topic throughout your essay.
- Use appropriate vocabulary and correct sentence structure.
- Develop each paragraph fully before moving to the next.
- End with a conclusion that reinforces your main ideas.
- Proofread your work to eliminate grammatical, spelling and punctuation errors.
