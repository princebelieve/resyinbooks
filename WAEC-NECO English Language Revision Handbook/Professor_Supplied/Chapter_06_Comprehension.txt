Chapter Six

Comprehension

Learning Objectives

At the end of this chapter, students should be able to:

- Explain the meaning and purpose of comprehension.
- Apply effective reading techniques.
- Identify the main ideas and supporting details in a passage.
- Infer the meanings of unfamiliar words from context.
- Answer WAEC and NECO comprehension questions accurately.

---

6.1 Meaning of Comprehension

Comprehension is the ability to read, understand, interpret and evaluate a written passage. It goes beyond merely recognising words; it involves understanding the writer's message and responding correctly to questions based on the passage.

In WAEC and NECO examinations, comprehension tests a candidate's ability to:

- Understand facts and ideas.
- Draw logical conclusions.
- Interpret vocabulary in context.
- Identify the writer's purpose and attitude.
- Summarise information accurately.

---

6.2 Types of Comprehension Questions

Candidates may encounter the following types of questions:

(a) Factual Questions

These require answers directly stated in the passage.

Example

Where did the writer spend his holiday?

---

(b) Inferential Questions

These require candidates to draw conclusions from information provided.

Example

Why was the principal disappointed with the students?

---

(c) Vocabulary in Context

Candidates explain the meaning of a word or expression as used in the passage.

Example

What does the word "resilient" mean as used in the passage?

---

(d) Writer's Attitude or Tone

These questions ask candidates to identify the writer's feelings or opinions.

Examples include:

- optimistic
- pessimistic
- humorous
- sympathetic
- critical
- objective

---

6.3 Reading Techniques

Successful candidates usually follow these steps:

Skimming

Read quickly to obtain the general idea of the passage.

Scanning

Read carefully to locate specific information.

Intensive Reading

Study the passage in detail to understand important points.

Critical Reading

Analyse the writer's arguments before answering questions.

---

6.4 Steps for Answering Comprehension Questions

1. Read the title carefully.
2. Read the entire passage once.
3. Read the questions before reading the passage again.
4. Underline important words and ideas.
5. Answer only from information contained in the passage.
6. Use your own words where required.
7. Avoid copying long sections unnecessarily.
8. Review your answers before moving to the next section.

---

6.5 Vocabulary in Context

The meaning of a word depends on how it is used.

Example

"The farmer was diligent, working from sunrise until sunset."

Here, diligent means hardworking.

Another Example

"The governor's decision was controversial."

Here, controversial means causing disagreement or public debate.

---

6.6 Sample Comprehension Passage

Read the passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

Ade was known throughout his village for his honesty and hard work. Although his family was poor, he remained determined to complete his education. Every morning before going to school, he helped his parents on the farm. In the evenings, he studied under the light of a kerosene lamp. His perseverance eventually earned him a scholarship to study at a university.

---

Questions

1. Why was Ade respected in his village?
2. What challenge did his family face?
3. How did Ade assist his parents?
4. What reward did he eventually receive?
5. What does the word perseverance mean as used in the passage?

---

Suggested Answers

1. He was respected because of his honesty and hard work.
2. His family was poor.
3. He helped them on the farm before going to school.
4. He received a university scholarship.
5. Perseverance means determination despite difficulties.

---

6.7 Common Mistakes Made by Candidates

- Reading too quickly.
- Ignoring important details.
- Introducing personal opinions not found in the passage.
- Copying entire paragraphs instead of answering precisely.
- Misinterpreting vocabulary.
- Poor time management.

---

6.8 Examination Tips

- Read every passage at least twice.
- Pay attention to signal words such as however, therefore, although and because.
- Use complete but concise answers.
- Where instructed, answer in your own words.
- Check your grammar and spelling before submitting your script.

---

Chapter Summary

Comprehension tests a student's ability to understand, analyse and interpret written passages. Regular reading, vocabulary development and constant practice with comprehension exercises are essential for success in WAEC and NECO English Language examinations.

WAEC/NECO Revision Questions

Objective Questions

Choose the correct answer.

1. Comprehension primarily tests a candidate's ability to:
   a. Memorise passages
   b. Understand written information
   c. Translate languages
   d. Write essays

2. Skimming is used to:
   a. Correct grammar
   b. Obtain the general idea of a passage
   c. Memorise vocabulary
   d. Write summaries

3. Vocabulary in context requires candidates to:
   a. Copy dictionary meanings
   b. Explain a word as used in the passage
   c. Spell difficult words
   d. Identify punctuation marks

4. Which reading technique helps locate specific information quickly?
   a. Skimming
   b. Scanning
   c. Guessing
   d. Storytelling

5. Which of the following should be avoided in comprehension answers?
   a. Using information from the passage
   b. Giving concise answers
   c. Introducing personal opinions not supported by the passage
   d. Reading the questions carefully

---

Theory Questions

1. Define comprehension.
2. Explain four types of comprehension questions.
3. Describe the steps involved in answering comprehension questions effectively.
4. Discuss five common mistakes made by candidates in comprehension exercises.
5. Explain the differences between skimming, scanning and intensive reading.

Practical Exercise

Read the following passage and answer the questions.

Passage

Ngozi always believed that success comes through discipline and hard work. Rather than spending her evenings on social media, she devoted her time to reading and completing assignments. Her determination inspired many of her classmates to improve their study habits. At the end of the academic year, she emerged as the overall best graduating student.

Questions

1. What did Ngozi believe was the key to success?
2. How did she spend her evenings?
3. What effect did her behaviour have on her classmates?
4. What achievement did she attain?
5. Explain the meaning of discipline as used in the passage.

---

WAEC/NECO Examination Tips

- Develop the habit of reading newspapers, novels and academic materials regularly.
- Learn at least five new English words every day and use them in sentences.
- Practise comprehension passages under timed conditions.
- Always support your answers with information from the passage rather than personal opinions.
