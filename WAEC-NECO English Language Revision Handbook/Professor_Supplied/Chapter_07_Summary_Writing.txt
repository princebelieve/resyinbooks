Chapter Seven

Summary Writing

Learning Objectives

At the end of this chapter, students should be able to:

- Define summary writing.
- Explain the importance of summary writing.
- Identify the main ideas in a passage.
- Distinguish between main points and supporting details.
- Write concise summaries in their own words.
- Answer WAEC and NECO summary questions effectively.

---

7.1 Meaning of Summary Writing

Summary writing is the process of presenting the main ideas of a passage in a brief, clear and accurate form without changing the original meaning.

A good summary contains only the essential points and excludes examples, illustrations, repetitions and unnecessary details.

---

7.2 Importance of Summary Writing

Summary writing helps students to:

- Identify important information.
- Improve reading and comprehension skills.
- Develop clear and concise writing.
- Organise ideas logically.
- Prepare effectively for WAEC and NECO examinations.
- Communicate information accurately.

---

7.3 Features of a Good Summary

A good summary should:

- Be brief and precise.
- Contain only the main ideas.
- Be written in the candidate's own words where required.
- Preserve the original meaning.
- Follow a logical order.
- Be grammatically correct.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.

---

7.4 Steps in Writing a Good Summary

Step 1: Read the Passage Carefully

Read the passage at least twice to understand the central message.

Step 2: Identify the Main Ideas

Underline or note the key points in each paragraph.

Step 3: Eliminate Minor Details

Ignore illustrations, anecdotes, examples and repeated ideas.

Step 4: Rewrite the Main Ideas

Express the ideas briefly using your own words whenever instructed.

Step 5: Revise Your Work

Check for grammar, spelling, punctuation and completeness.

---

7.5 Main Ideas and Supporting Details

Main Idea

The central message of a paragraph.

Supporting Details

Facts, examples or explanations that develop the main idea.

Example

Paragraph:

"Regular exercise improves physical health, strengthens the heart, reduces stress and increases energy."

Main Idea:

Regular exercise improves health.

Supporting Details:

- Strengthens the heart.
- Reduces stress.
- Increases energy.

---

7.6 Common Errors in Summary Writing

Candidates often:

- Copy long sentences directly from the passage.
- Include unnecessary examples.
- Introduce personal opinions.
- Omit important points.
- Write excessively long answers.
- Ignore the examiner's instructions.

---

7.7 Sample Summary Exercise

Passage

Many successful students plan their daily activities carefully. They prepare study timetables, attend classes regularly, complete assignments promptly and revise their lessons consistently. They also avoid distractions such as excessive television viewing and unnecessary use of social media.

---

Questions

1. In one sentence, state how successful students organise their studies.

2. In one sentence, state what successful students avoid.

---

Suggested Answers

1. Successful students organise their studies through careful planning, regular attendance, prompt completion of assignments and consistent revision.

2. Successful students avoid distractions such as excessive television viewing and unnecessary use of social media.

---

7.8 Examination Techniques

To perform well:

- Read every question before reading the passage again.
- Note the number of points required.
- Answer only what is asked.
- Use simple and correct English.
- Write complete sentences unless instructed otherwise.
- Observe any specified word limit.
- Revise your answers before submitting your script.

---

7.9 Difference Between Comprehension and Summary

Comprehension| Summary
Tests understanding of a passage| Tests ability to condense information
May require detailed answers| Requires brief answers
Includes vocabulary questions| Focuses on main ideas
May include inference| Emphasises concise expression

---

Chapter Summary

Summary writing requires candidates to identify the essential ideas in a passage and present them briefly, accurately and logically. Success depends on careful reading, selecting only the key points and expressing them clearly in concise English.

WAEC/NECO Revision Questions

Objective Questions

Choose the correct answer.

1. Summary writing mainly involves:
   a. Copying the passage
   b. Identifying the main ideas
   c. Memorising paragraphs
   d. Giving personal opinions

2. A good summary should be:
   a. Long and detailed
   b. Brief and accurate
   c. Emotional
   d. Repetitive

3. Which of the following should be omitted from a summary?
   a. Main ideas
   b. Essential facts
   c. Unnecessary examples
   d. Key points

4. Summary answers should generally be written:
   a. In the candidate's own words where required
   b. Exactly as they appear in the passage
   c. In note form only
   d. As personal opinions

5. Which quality is most important in summary writing?
   a. Brevity
   b. Length
   c. Ornamentation
   d. Repetition

Practical Exercise

Read the passage below and answer the questions.

Passage

A clean environment contributes to good health and improved quality of life. Individuals should dispose of refuse properly, keep their surroundings tidy, clear blocked drains and plant trees where possible. Governments should also provide efficient waste disposal systems and educate citizens on environmental sanitation.

Questions

1. In one sentence, state how individuals can maintain a clean environment.

2. In one sentence, state the responsibilities of government in maintaining environmental sanitation.

---

WAEC/NECO Examination Tips

- Read the passage more than once before attempting the questions.
- Underline the key points in each paragraph.
- Exclude illustrations, anecdotes and repeated information.
- Express answers clearly and concisely.
- Adhere strictly to the examiner's instructions regarding the number of points or sentences required.
- Practise summary exercises regularly using past WAEC and NECO examination questions.
