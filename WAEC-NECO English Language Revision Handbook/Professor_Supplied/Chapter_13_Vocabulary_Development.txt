Chapter Thirteen

Vocabulary Development

Learning Objectives

At the end of this chapter, students should be able to:

- Explain the meaning and importance of vocabulary.
- Identify root words, prefixes and suffixes.
- Form new words correctly.
- Distinguish between commonly confused words.
- Improve spelling and word usage.
- Develop strategies for expanding vocabulary.

---

13.1 Meaning of Vocabulary

Vocabulary is the collection of words a person understands and uses in speaking, reading and writing.

A rich vocabulary enables students to express themselves clearly and perform well in comprehension, summary, essay writing, letter writing and Lexis and Structure.

---

13.2 Importance of Vocabulary Development

A good vocabulary helps students to:

- Communicate effectively.
- Improve reading comprehension.
- Write better essays and letters.
- Understand examination questions.
- Increase confidence in speaking and writing.
- Perform better in WAEC and NECO examinations.

---

13.3 Root Words

A root word is the basic form of a word from which other words are formed.

Root Word| Derived Words
act| action, active, actor, activity
employ| employee, employer, employment
govern| government, governor, governance
educate| education, educator, educational
create| creation, creative, creativity
decide| decision, decisive, indecisive
move| movement, movable, immovable
develop| development, developer, developmental
organise| organisation, organiser, organised
nation| national, nationality, international

---

13.4 Prefixes

A prefix is added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning.

Prefix| Meaning| Example
un-| not| unhappy
dis-| opposite| disagree
re-| again| rewrite
pre-| before| preview
mis-| wrongly| mislead
non-| not| non-smoker
inter-| between| international
anti-| against| anti-corruption
sub-| under| submarine
over-| excessively| overwork

---

13.5 Suffixes

A suffix is added to the end of a word to form a new word or change its grammatical function.

Suffix| Example
-er| teacher
-or| actor
-ness| kindness
-ment| development
-tion| education
-ity| honesty
-ful| beautiful
-less| hopeless
-able| readable
-ly| carefully

---

13.6 Compound Words

Compound words are formed by joining two or more words.

Examples:

- Blackboard
- Football
- Classroom
- Headmaster
- Newspaper
- Airport
- Rainfall
- Handbag
- Bedroom
- Toothpaste

---

13.7 Commonly Confused Words

Word| Meaning

---

13.10 Strategies for Vocabulary Development

Students can improve their vocabulary by:

- Reading newspapers daily.
- Reading novels and biographies.
- Keeping a personal vocabulary notebook.
- Using a dictionary regularly.
- Learning five to ten new words every day.
- Solving crossword puzzles and word games.
- Listening to educated speakers.
- Using newly learnt words in conversation and writing.

---

13.11 Contextual Meaning

Many English words have different meanings depending on context.

Example:

"The bank approved my loan."

Here, bank means a financial institution.

"The fisherman sat on the bank of the river."

Here, bank means the side of a river.

Always use the context to determine meaning.

---

13.12 Examination Tips

- Learn new words every day.
- Revise prefixes and suffixes regularly.
- Practise correct spelling.
- Read widely.
- Use a good English dictionary.
- Answer vocabulary questions according to the context provided.

---

Chapter Summary

Vocabulary development is a continuous process that enhances communication and academic performance. A broad vocabulary, combined with correct spelling and an understanding of word formation, enables students to excel in every section of the WAEC and NECO English Language examinations.

WAEC/NECO Revision Questions

Objective Questions

Choose the correct answer.

1. A prefix is added:
   a. at the end of a word
   b. in the middle of a word
   c. at the beginning of a word
   d. after punctuation

2. Which of the following contains a suffix?
   a. unhappy
   b. rewrite
   c. kindness
   d. preview

3. Which word is correctly spelt?
   a. Goverment
   b. Government
   c. Government
   d. Govermant

4. Which word means "to receive"?
   a. Except
   b. Accept
   c. Affect
   d. Effect

5. A person who studies plants is called a:
   a. Zoologist
   b. Botanist
   c. Astronomer
   d. Pharmacist

---

Theory Questions

1. Define vocabulary.
2. Explain the importance of vocabulary development.
3. Distinguish between:
   - Prefix and suffix.
   - Root word and derived word.
4. Explain five strategies for improving vocabulary.
5. Differentiate between the following pairs of words and use each in a sentence:
   - Accept / Except
   - Advice / Advise
   - Principal / Principle
   - Affect / Effect
   - Stationary / Stationery

---

Practical Exercise

A. Form new words by adding suitable prefixes or suffixes to the following root words:

1. Happy
2. Read
3. Employ
4. Honest
5. Organise

B. Replace the expressions below with one word.

1. One who studies the stars.
2. One who prepares medicines.
3. One who cannot read or write.
4. One who loves mankind.
5. One who writes books.

C. Choose the correct word to complete each sentence.

1. The teacher gave me useful ________. (advice / advise)
2. The school ________ addressed the students. (principal / principle)
3. We should not allow failure to ________ our determination. (affect / effect)
4. The office ordered new ________. (stationery / stationary)
5. She decided to ________ the invitation. (accept / except)

---

WAEC/NECO Examination Tips

- Expand your vocabulary through consistent reading and regular use of a dictionary.
- Study word families, prefixes and suffixes to recognise unfamiliar words.
- Learn the meanings of commonly confused words and practise using them correctly.
- Pay attention to spelling and context when answering examination questions.
- Revise past WAEC and NECO vocabulary questions to reinforce your understanding.
